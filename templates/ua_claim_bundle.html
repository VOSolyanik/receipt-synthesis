{# ONE FILE, TWO DOCUMENTS — the documents of one claim, stitched into a single file the way a claimant submits them. A COMPOSITION TEMPLATE AND NOT A DOCUMENT CLASS, which is the whole of what distinguishes it from every other file here. It is not registered in `ARCHETYPES`, nothing plans it, no builder produces it and no label names it — because it is not a document: it is the FILE two documents are carried in. `assembler._compose_bundle` renders it, from the clean renders of the documents themselves; see `assembler.documents_share_one_file` for when. ⚠️ IT WAS A MOCK-UP AND IS NOT ONE ANY MORE. What it demonstrated by hand — that a segmentation step needs a file whose contents it has to disagree with — the corpus now contains: a share of eligible claims declared in `file_composition.bundle_share` (config/generation.yaml) arrives as one of these. NO `data-field` MARKERS, and their absence is structural rather than an omission. The fields of a bundled document are inside the embedded image, and their boxes come from THAT document's own render, offset into this file's coordinates by its region's origin. A marker here would add a second, empty box under a name the document already uses. 🔴 THE OTHER HALF OF THE SEGMENTATION CONTROL. `ua_bank_statement` breaks «one page = one document» in one direction: one document across two sheets. This breaks it in the other: two documents in one file. A file-splitting step measured on a corpus containing only the first would still be scoring against a guarantee — every cut it needs to make would be a cut it never has to refuse. Both directions or neither. 👁 AND IT IS AN OBSERVED PATTERN RATHER THAN A HYPOTHESIS: claimants really do staple the documents of one claim into a single PDF before submitting them. ⛔ DOCUMENTS OF ONE CLAIM ONLY. A file holding documents of DIFFERENT claims is a shape this generator does not produce, deliberately; the reason is at the bundling site. 🔴 THE STITCHING ADDS NOTHING, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE DIFFICULTY. Compare a paginated statement: it repeats its own column headings and prints «Сторінка N з M», so the document tells you which sheets belong together. Here there is no cover page, no continuous pagination, no unifying header and no page numbering across the file. Each sheet carries only its own document's marks, and **nothing on the file says the two are different documents**. A segmenter has nothing to key on but the documents themselves. 🔴 NEITHER BODY IS REWRITTEN OR COPIED. Each sheet is the PNG of a shipped archetype, rendered from a shipped builder's context by the same renderer, embedded at its natural size. What that buys is that the pages of this file ARE the documents the dataset already contains, so a segmentation result on it is a result about those documents rather than about two images written to resemble them. #} {# ONE `data-document` FOR THE WHOLE FILE. The renderer frames the image on this element, so the image is the FILE — which is the unit a splitting step is given, and the unit whose contents it has to disagree with. A root per sheet would have answered the question the archetype asks. #}
