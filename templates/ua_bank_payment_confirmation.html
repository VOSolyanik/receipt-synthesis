{# Ukrainian bank payment confirmation — квитанція про оплату / платіжна інструкція. A DIFFERENT DOCUMENT CLASS FROM THE FISCAL RECEIPTS, not another width of one: A4 rather than a till roll, issued by a bank rather than by a cash register, and 👁 8/8 observed confirmations carry NO TABLE OF ITEMS. It therefore shares no body with them and has its own stylesheet; `templates/ua_fiscal_receipt.jinja` is deliberately not included. ONE TEMPLATE WITH A CONDITIONAL BLOCK, AND NOT TWO ARCHETYPES. The earlier plan split this class into a quittance carrying a payment purpose and a card slip carrying an authorization code. 👁 3 of 8 documents carry a masked card AND an authorization code AND a purpose at once, which refutes the split outright. What varies is HOW THE PAYMENT WAS INITIATED — `payment_confirmation.initiation` in config/fiscal-rules.yaml — and the conditionals below are that one axis. Everything else on the page is present or absent at its observed rate. ONE LAYOUT, AND THE LIMIT IS DECLARED. Three real banks were observed and their layouts differ; this is one arrangement of the fields they all carry. Reproducing three layouts would mean inventing two of them from a sample of two or three documents each, so field-set variation is modelled and visual variation is a named gap — `known_limitations` in config/labelling-schema.yaml. Every element a consumer would extract carries data-field="". A field that is not on THIS document is omitted entirely rather than rendered empty: an empty element leaves a bounding box pointing at nothing. The two forms of EMPTINESS 👁 observed on real documents are a different matter and both are produced — a hyphen printed as the payer's value, and a caption with nothing under it for the recipient's bank — because an extractor returns a string for the first and nothing for the second, and a corpus that collapsed them would make accuracy on empty fields unmeasurable. #} {# The document root wraps the shared body at its content's own size: the renderer frames the image to this element, and `fit-content` keeps its extent the A4 sheet's rather than the browser window's. The body itself carries no root — see its own header. #}
