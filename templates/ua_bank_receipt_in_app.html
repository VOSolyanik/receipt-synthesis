See templates/README.md. 🔴 WHY THIS IS AN ARCHETYPE AND NOT A DEGRADATION SETTING. It tests, directly and cheaply, that THE MEDIUM DOES NOT CHANGE THE GROUND TRUTH. One document, two carriers — an A4 sheet and a phone screen — and the labels have to come out the same. Nothing else in this repository asks that question: `degrader` DAMAGES an image, whereas this REFRAMES the document, and a frame is what a classifier is most likely to read as a different kind of thing. 🔴 THE BODY IS NOT REWRITTEN, AND NOT COPIED EITHER. The white card holds `ua_bank_payment_confirmation` — the shipped template, rendered by the shipped renderer from the shipped builder's context — embedded as a frame. Copying its markup into a second file would create two spellings of one layout, which is the defect the labelling contract exists to prevent; extracting a shared body out of it would edit a file a shipped archetype renders from, which a mock-up on a side branch may not do. The frame is what is left, and it has a property neither of the other routes has: THE INNER DOCUMENT IS THE ONE THE DATASET ALREADY CONTAINS, so "the labels must agree" stops being an assumption about two similar files. ⚠️ AND IT IS A COST, STATED RATHER THAN HIDDEN. Connecting this archetype means the renderer learns to render a document INSIDE another one: the inner page has to be written to disk before the outer page can point at it, which `Renderer.render` does not do today — the render script beside this repository does it instead. That is a real decision for whoever connects it, and the alternative — extracting the confirmation's body into a shared `.jinja` fragment, exactly as the three fiscal receipts already share `ua_fiscal_receipt.jinja` — is the cheaper one at that point. It is not taken here because it changes a shipped file.
