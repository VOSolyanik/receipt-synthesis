{# Ukrainian voluntary health insurance contract — договір добровільного медичного страхування. THREE PAGES IN ONE FILE, and that is the whole reason it exists. ⚠️ A MOCK-UP, on the same terms as the others in this directory: not registered in `ARCHETYPES`, no builder, no run, and NO `data-field` attributes — so the renderer collects no bounding box and nothing here becomes a label. See templates/README.md. 🔴 WHY: «ONE PAGE = ONE DOCUMENT» IS CURRENTLY TRUE BY CONSTRUCTION. Every archetype in this repository renders exactly one page, so a corpus built from them cannot contradict that rule — and a later step that splits a submitted file into logical documents would score a perfect result on it while never having been tested. A measurement whose ideal is guaranteed by the construction of the data measures the construction, not the system. This document is the control that makes the rule falsifiable: ONE document, THREE pages. ⛔ AND IT IS EXPLICITLY NOT EVIDENCE. `medical_insurance` in config/policy.yaml states in as many words that a claim is proven by a bank payment confirmation whose purpose names the policy, and that «the policy document itself is out of scope and is never parsed». So this belongs to the catch-all class and to nothing else. It exists to be SEGMENTED and CLASSIFIED, never to be read for a verdict — and 🔴 the generator cannot even label it as the catch-all today: `DocType` in schemas.py has no `other` member and no `contract` member, which is RC-14, still open. WHAT MAKES THE PAGE COUNT EVIDENCED RATHER THAN CHOSEN. 📄 Article 89(2) of the law on insurance (№ 1909-IX) lists NINETEEN particulars an insurance contract must contain — among them the list of insured risks, the list of exclusions, the payout procedure, the grounds for refusing a payout, the parties' rights and obligations, the amendment and termination procedure and the dispute-resolution procedure. That is not a page of content. The layout below places those nineteen across three sheets, which is what they come to; a one-page insurance contract would be the invention here, not a three-page one. 📄 A SECOND, INDEPENDENT SOURCE decides what the document is called. Article 979 of the Civil Code makes the contract the thing and allows it to be ISSUED as a поліс or сертифікат — so the поліс is a FORM of the contract rather than a separate document class. The title printed here is therefore the contract's, and «поліс» appears nowhere: naming it both would suggest the corpus holds two classes where the law holds one. ⚠️ THE CLAUSE PROSE IS INVENTED AND SHORT. What is modelled is the PAGE STRUCTURE and the placement of the required particulars — which is what a segmenter and a classifier read. Real contract prose runs far longer, and a mock-up that padded itself to look right would be inventing legal text at length to no measurable end. The limit is declared in templates/README.md rather than left to be discovered from a render. #} {# ONE `data-document` FOR THREE SHEETS, and the placement is the point rather than a detail.
