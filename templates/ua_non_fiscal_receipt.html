{# Ukrainian NON-FISCAL sales slip — товарний чек, issued without a cash register. SHIPPED. It is registered in `ARCHETYPES` as `ua_non_fiscal_receipt`, built by `content_builder.build_non_fiscal_receipt`, and a run contains one wherever a claim was planned as `not_proof_of_payment`. Every element a consumer would extract carries data-field="", exactly as the fiscal body does. ⛔ AND NO FIELD NAMES THE NEGATIVE MARKER, which is a decision rather than an omission left over from the mock-up. What this page carries is a positive TITLE plus an ABSENCE of fiscal requisites, and the contract's marker field is shaped as "marker text with its position in the text" — a shape an absence cannot fill. That is RC-08 in config/labelling-schema.yaml, and it is open. The absence is still ground truth: it is recorded as `has_fiscal_number`, `has_qr` and `qr_is_fiscal`, all false, on a page that otherwise reads like a fiscal receipt. WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS FOR. The consuming system's rule is that a NEGATIVE fiscality marker overrides every positive fiscality signal a document carries. Until this archetype was connected nothing in the corpus carried anything negative to override with, so the rule had no test document — the whole argument rested on a class the corpus did not contain. WHAT THE SOURCES ACTUALLY SAY, AND WHY THIS PAGE LOOKS THE WAY IT DOES. 📄 The form and content of a товарний чек is NOT defined by law — that is stated by the tax service itself, and it is why this is a mock-up of a convention rather than of a form. What the tax service does state (БЗ 109.10, quoted identically by four independent outlets) is a MINIMUM: the document must carry the wording «Товарний чек», and its content must satisfy п. 2 розд. II of the Положення про форму та зміст розрахункових документів (наказ Мінфіну № 13 від 21.01.2016) — the fiscal receipt's own form — EXCEPT for the fiscal number of the register and the wording «ФІСКАЛЬНИЙ ЧЕК». 🔴 SO THE NEGATIVE MARKER IS NOT THE STRING THE CONTRACT ASSUMES. The contract names «НЕ ФІСКАЛЬНИЙ ЧЕК» as the Ukrainian negative marker. No public source found says that string is printed on a SALES document. What the sources evidence is two other things, and both are printed here: * a POSITIVE SELF-IDENTIFYING TITLE — «ТОВАРНИЙ ЧЕК» — standing where «ФІСКАЛЬНИЙ ЧЕК» would stand on the fiscal form; * an ABSENCE — no fiscal number, no fiscal wording, no register's maker, no online marker, no QR. The contract's own vocabulary already admits an absence as one kind of negative marker ("an empty receipt-number field"), so this is inside the rule rather than beside it. A SECOND LEGAL SOURCE, INDEPENDENT OF THE FIRST, decides the two lines at the foot. A товарний чек is a primary accounting document, so ст. 9 of the law on accounting (№ 996-XIV) requires it to name the person responsible for the operation and to carry that person's signature. A fiscal receipt carries neither. They are printed here for that reason and no other. ⛔ NOT MODELLED, and named so it is not read off the render: the HANDWRITTEN товарний чек on a pre-printed pad, which is the other real variety and a different image problem entirely. Body deliberately NOT shared with templates/ua_fiscal_receipt.jinja. The shared body prints the fiscal identity unconditionally, and making it conditional would edit a file three shipped archetypes render from — a change to the corpus, made from a mock-up. The stylesheet IS shared: `ua_fiscal_receipt.css` carries the till-roll rules, and a second copy of them would be a second thing to keep in step. #} {# One document root, as every template in this directory declares. #}
